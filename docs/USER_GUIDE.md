# User Guide — Smart Placement Readiness Analyser

## Step 1: Review Overview & Transparency Notice
Open the application to view the **Overview** screen. Review the four assessment pillars and note that readiness scores are educational guidance, not hiring guarantees.

## Step 2: Explore Target Roles & Skill Weights
Navigate to **Target Roles & Skills** to inspect the 6 supported roles (`Software Developer`, `Python Developer`, `Data Analyst`, `AI/ML Engineer`, `Web Developer`, `QA Engineer`). Click **Inspect Skill Weights** to see each required skill's percentage weight, target score (`65%–75%`), and rationale.

## Step 3: Create Your Student Profile
Navigate to **Student Profile**, enter your display name, education level, target job role, current skills, preferred programming languages, weekly available study hours (`1–80`), and preparation goal, then click **Save Student Profile**.

## Step 4: Complete Diagnostic Assessments
Open the **Assessment Centre** and start an attempt for any active assessment:
- **Aptitude & Logical Reasoning**
- **Programming & Core CS Fundamentals**
- **Role-Specific Technical Concepts**
- **Communication, Interview & Portfolio Readiness**

Navigate between questions using the numbered pills or **Previous / Next** buttons, then click **Review & Submit Assessment** -> **Confirm Submit**.

## Step 5: Inspect Results & Skill Gaps
After submission, the **Results & Skill Gaps** screen displays:
- Your overall assessment percentage and points earned.
- Evidence-backed strengths (`>= 70%`) and priority improvement areas (`< 70%`).
- Question-by-question grading feedback and conceptual explanations.

## Step 6: Follow Your Personalised Learning Plan
Open **Learning Plan** to view recommended activities ordered by priority (`High`, `Medium`, `Low`). Update each recommendation's status (`Pending`, `In Progress`, `Completed`, `Dismissed`) as you progress.

## Step 7: Track Progress & Retake Assessments
Open **Progress Tracker** to log self-study practice sessions, inspect skill score progression from initial to latest attempts, and view version-validated retake comparisons.
